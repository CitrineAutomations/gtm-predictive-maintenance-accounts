# Approach

Draft answers to three questions: how to pick the 250 accounts, who the SDRs reach first, and how to know in 60 days if it works. Items marked (assumption) need the vendor's CRM data to confirm.

## 1. How to pick the 250

1. Roll plants and divisions up to the parent company. Flag parents that already have a customer plant.
2. Rank parents, not plants.

Signals that matter:

- Plant count under one parent, in asset-heavy industries. More plants means more room to spread.
- Plant-level triggers: new or expanded capacity, open maintenance and reliability roles.
- Signs of connected equipment.

Signals to ignore:

- Revenue alone. The last list used it and got near-zero replies.
- Generic intent signals that cannot be tied to a plant.
- Downtime-cost statistics. They are vendor claims. Use them in messaging, not in the score.

Reserve part of the 250 for parents that have one customer plant. (assumption)

## 2. Who the SDRs reach first

- Plant operations or maintenance lead at one plant. The best wins started at one plant.
- IT and procurement join later in the nine-month cycle.
- If the parent already has a customer plant, do not cold-open at headquarters. Ask the current plant's champion for an intro to sister plants. Lead with that plant's results.

## 3. How to know in 60 days

Closed deals will not show yet. Track leading signals:

- Reply and meeting rate against the old list.
- Meetings with plant operations roles.
- Number of parents with a second plant engaged.
- A control group of comparable accounts that gets no change.

At day 30, swap out accounts with no activity. Ask the SDRs which accounts feel wrong.

## Known limits of v0

- Public data only. No CRM, no customer flags.
- EPA TRI covers only plants that report toxic releases. This skews the industry mix.
- Signal weights are assumptions.
